## Career arc

**Tech leadership in Genetics (2005-2011).** At DNA Direct, a direct-to-consumer genetic testing startup, I headed up technology in the company's early stages. In addition to owning the Java/Spring/Hibernate platform end to end: patient portal, e-commerce, internal CRM. I also hired and managed technical vendors, vetted and implemented 3rd-party solutions like website analytics, and represented the technology in Series B investor discussions. When Medco Health Solutions acquired DNA Direct in 2010, I moved into product and built pharmacogenomic decision-support tools that integrated with Medco enterprise software.

**Healthcare from the inside (2011-2019).** I wanted to understand why genetic tests weren't standard of care, so I went to the side of healthcare that moves the data. I led interface work for 900+ providers at Tenet, including the interface migration for the Tenet-Baylor Scott & White merger. At Santa Clara Valley Medical Center I built Epic integrations for lab and device systems.

**Natera (2019-2026).** I joined as a technical product manager and was promoted to Associate Director, Technical Product Management.

- Launched Prospera, Rare Disease and the Austin lab on schedule.
- Led the move of Natera's core LIMS from a legacy monolith to event-driven microservices.
- OCR-driven accessioning cut kit processing time by 60-70%.
- A Temporal-based Report Management System cut report template changes from 4 weeks to 1.
- Grew the platform product team from 3 to 14, with a reporting manager layer.
- Built a prioritization framework that Engineering later adopted as the base of its project planning process.
